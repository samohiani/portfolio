## Patterns across the references

1. Very strong, high-contrast typography establishes the person's role quickly.
2. Work appears close to the opening view, with images or case studies that show actual output.
3. A restrained palette lets one graphic moment or accent colour carry the mood.
4. Editorial composition and asymmetry create character; repeated identical cards are less important.
5. Interaction is concentrated in a few useful moments, such as exploring projects or experience.

Samuel's preference after reviewing these references: the overall site should be minimalist, polished and cinematic. The Stranger Things influence should remain a small moment, possibly a loading animation, rather than control the whole visual system.

## Current portfolio comparison

The local site currently leads with a cream background, blue highlighted headline, numbered section labels, and a large interactive stack card. Its content is substantial, but its opening mood is lighter and more conventional than these references. The selected projects already include live work and technical details worth keeping prominent.

## Possible Stranger Things influence to test later

Test a brief cinematic loading moment with restrained deep red light or subtle grain, then reveal a clear, quiet portfolio. Keep engineering work legible and avoid copying the show's logo or title treatment. Respect reduced-motion settings and do not make visitors wait through a long intro.
