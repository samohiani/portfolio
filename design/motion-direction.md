# Motion direction: signals and state

The portfolio shows work on interfaces and payment systems. Its movement should suggest a clear change of state: arrival, navigation progress, project selection, and a completed contact action. The red signal from the opening is the consistent accent.

## Sequence

- The existing cinematic loader hands off to a two-line hero reveal, followed by its introduction and current-role note.
- A thin red line tracks reading progress at the top of the viewport.
- Section rules briefly trace red as their sections enter view. Experience rows settle once as the chronology is reached.
- The selected project advances every 6.5 seconds only while the work section is visible. Hover or keyboard focus pauses it. Manual selection stops autoplay; a visible control resumes it.
- Project changes use the existing image wipe with a short scan line. The images retain their full aspect ratio.

## Restraint check

There is no forced scrolling, continuous parallax, moving background texture, or repeated fade-up on every paragraph. Each animation either marks a state change or helps the reader orient. Reduced-motion settings remove automatic cycling and decorative transitions.
