# Motion direction: signals and state

The portfolio shows work on interfaces and payment systems. Its movement should suggest a clear change of state: arrival, navigation progress, project selection, and a completed contact action. The red signal from the opening is the consistent accent.

## Sequence

- The cinematic title card draws a red horizon, then opens along a vertical rift into the two-line hero reveal. It runs once per session and can be replayed with `?intro=1`.
- The rift remains as a low-intensity red light in the hero. Its haze and small embers move slowly, while the title, links, and current-role note stay clear and readable.
- A thin red line tracks reading progress at the top of the viewport.
- Section rules and titles briefly trace into view. Experience rows settle once as the chronology is reached; their top line responds to hover.
- The selected project advances every 6.5 seconds only while the work section is visible. Hover or keyboard focus pauses it. Manual selection stops autoplay; a visible control resumes it.
- Project changes use the existing image wipe with a short scan line. The images retain their full aspect ratio.

## Restraint check

There is no forced scrolling, continuous parallax, strobe effect, or repeated fade-up on every paragraph. The ambient hero light is deliberately slow. Reduced-motion settings remove the loader, ambient movement, automatic cycling, and decorative transitions.
