# Cinematic portfolio direction

The opening sequence and Nuvion visual were revised after Samuel's next review; see [motion and project-visual revision](./motion-direction.md) for the current treatment.

Samuel asked for a more sophisticated, complete design pass after reviewing the light portfolio and Dave Oratokhai's dark portfolio. The reference contributes a sense of atmosphere, a confident display face, a bespoke central visual, and a staged entrance. This design uses Samuel's payment infrastructure work as its own subject matter; it does not reuse the reference's sword, Japanese lettering, layout, or assets.

## Tokens

| Role | Value | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Void | `#090A0D` | Main canvas and loader. |
| Panel | `#15171C` | Project stages and timeline surfaces. |
| Bone | `#F1F0EC` | Main text; a crisp neutral rather than warm cream. |
| Ash | `#A8A9AD` | Supporting text. |
| Trace | `#A62B37` | One controlled red signal. |
| Rule | `#303239` | Information boundaries. |

**Type:** Syne for names, section titles and project headlines; Manrope for descriptive text and navigation. The contrast is in shape and rhythm, not colour changes within a headline.

## Layout

```text
SO.                                      Work   Experience   About   Contact

       FROM INTERFACE
       TO INFRASTRUCTURE.       [payment-state artwork]
       role + concise position  [intent / authentication / response]
       view work / contact

       Selected work             four editorial case studies
       Nuvion / Rivo             custom visual evidence
       Qualiflow / Iraid         real product screenshots

       Experience                dated, readable timeline
       Earlier builds            compact archive
       About                     short first-person statement
       Contact                   decisive ending
```

Left alignment keeps the work easy to scan. The hero is intentionally asymmetrical: large typography balances one custom diagram. Project layouts vary to fit their evidence rather than using identical cards.

## One memorable sequence

The first visit opens on a dark field. A red trace draws through a simple `SO` monogram and reveals Samuel's name. The field parts to uncover the hero's payment-state visual. The animation is short, replayable for review, skipped on return visits in the same session, and absent for reduced-motion preferences. No fake percentage, spinner or loading claim.

## Generic-design audit before build

- Avoid the reference's Japanese motifs, sword, clock and pill navigation: they belong to Dave's identity.
- Avoid a grid of identical dark cards, decorative numeric labels and repeated glow effects.
- Make every line in the payment visual correspond to a real area of Samuel's acquiring work: intent, authentication and provider response.
- Keep all employer and impact claims from the confirmed content sheet; the Rivo dashboard screenshot remains unpublished.
